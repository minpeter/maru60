Independent passive power-sensitivity review

Verdict: PASS for synthetic charge-balance mathematics and replay only. NOT an active-regulator, current-board transient, stability, or manufacturing qualification.

All 18 archived decks replayed with the existing ngspice shared library. Replayed numerical arrays are identical to the originals. All samples are finite, time is strictly increasing, end times and reported extrema/endpoints match, and recorded deck/waveform SHA256 hashes match. Independent piecewise analytic comparison of every sample, including both capacitor and output nodes, has maximum error 1.006039 microvolts, below the stated 10 microvolt criterion. ESR node drop matches signed instantaneous current times 0.02 ohm to within 1 nanovolt.

Units/signs/timing are correct: 49.7/24.85/9.94 microfarads, a 0.2 ampere mismatch, 0.02 ohm ESR and a 10 ns ramp beginning at 5 us. Hold duration is measured from ramp START, not end; the simulation ends at 5 us + hold. Integrated charge is 0.2 A times (hold - 5 ns), and full ESR contribution is 4 mV. Load draws charge and lowers voltage; unload adds charge and raises voltage. No recovery is simulated after the fixed-current interval. UIC deliberately sets capacitor voltage to 4 V and skips operating-point solution; this is not startup verification.

Required provenance clarification: the original packet is genuinely generated against combined-trial PCB SHA256 56d66f65bb1f30d6ec7860f398cfd4f2399f3a9d305ef80d4bebab721d2571d9, NOT final corrected PCB SHA256 9d787421bddf23dd06eb8ec51a984ab6cbcc7a36b92a8700a041cb41fc1f18ed. Independent read-only pcbnew inspection confirms the final board has exactly the same four references, value strings, and +4V_REG/GND pad mappings. Thus its nameplate inventory remains applicable to this lumped example, but the packet must not be described as extracted or rerun from final layout. Preserve original hashes; add this equivalence cross-reference rather than relabel source provenance.

Scope statements are otherwise appropriately conservative. The 100/50/20 percent choices are hypothetical, not known component bounds. Large load/unload deviations are deliberately unclamped current-source results, not predicted rail voltages or safe operating limits. C140/C141 value text 'effective>=12uF TBD' is not evidence of effective capacitance. All active-regulator loop/current-limit, actual component DC-bias/effective-capacitance, ESR/ESL, layout, eFuse, actual load, startup/fault/thermal and pass/fail threshold qualifications remain missing. Prior official active-model compatibility failure is not resolved here. Do not translate 'all18_runs_and_analytic_checks_pass' into an electrical stability pass.
