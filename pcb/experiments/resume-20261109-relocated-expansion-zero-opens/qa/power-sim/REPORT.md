# Maru60 power-tree transient simulation

**Status: SIMULATION_ONLY. No stability, thermal, USB-compliance, EMC or hardware qualification.**

This report is generated by `pcb/tools/simulate_power.py`. It reads the
native netlist and the core schematic, asserts the power-tree connectivity and
values, and runs deterministic behavioral transient models. Every model
parameter that is not read from CAD is an explicit assumption in
`power-sim.json`.

## 1. eFuse dV/dt ramp

Model: TPS259470 dV/dt reference ramps at `SR = 2/C_dVdt[nF]` V/ms;
the pass FET is a voltage source behind 28.3 mohm with an ILIM clamp;
the protected rail is C126 1 uF + C136 100 nF + C137 4.7 uF, derated
0.92 at 5 V (5.336 uF effective) with a 2 mohm parallel ESR. R125 100 ohm
gives a 1 us RC on the dV/dt pin, negligible against the 25 ms ramp.

- Nominal no-load: 90% at **22.51 ms**, 5 V at **24.98 ms**.
- Capacitor-only inrush: **1.067 mA**; stored energy **66.7 uJ**.
- 0.3 A equivalent load: peak **301.1 mA**, still below the 425 mA minimum ILIM; final rail 4.9915 V.
- Current-limit test into 4 ohm (1 A at 4 V): steady current 500.0 mA at the 500 mA nominal ILIM, output settles at 2.00 V; the 400 us response is modeled as a first-order lag.

The 1.16 mA / 72.5 uJ analytical baseline used 5.8 uF nominal. The
simulation uses 5.336 uF effective, so the current is 1.067 mA and the
stored energy is 66.7 uJ. Both are far below the 425 mA minimum ILIM.

## 2. Buck TPS62932

Model: average peak-current-mode buck with L2 4.7 uH, DCR 20 mohm,
HS/LS 76/32 mohm, C100 4.7 uF + C120 1 uF + C140/C141 12 uF effective
each (29.7 uF total), 20 mohm ESR, 500 kHz, 98% duty clamp, and a
soft-start reference from C139 100 nF. The inner current loop is a
50 kHz first-order lag with physical inductor slew limiting; the outer
voltage loop is a PI compensator targeting 20 kHz crossover. **This is a
synthetic loop, not the TPS62932 internal compensation; no stability claim.**

- Startup 5 V / 0.1 A: 90% at **13.10 ms**, 99% at **14.41 ms**, overshoot **1.9 mV**, monotonic=True.
- Startup 5 V / 0.3 A: 90% at **13.10 ms**, overshoot **1.9 mV**, monotonic=True.
- Load step 0.1->0.3 A: undershoot **13.7 mV**, recovery to 0.2% **0.010 ms**, peak iL **0.339 A**.
- 0.2 A unload: overshoot **13.7 mV**, recovery to 0.2% **0.010 ms**, min iL **0.061 A**.
- Calculated inductor ripple: **340.4 mA**; output ripple estimate 6.8 mV ESR + 2.9 mV capacitive.

The load-step numbers are a function of the assumed 20 kHz loop. They
show the direction and scale of the response, not the TPS62932's actual
phase margin or transient response.

## 3. UVLO / OVLO

The sweep is 0 -> 6.5 -> 0 V over 2 s with the R120-R123 dividers and
the TPS25947 comparator thresholds. The state machine reproduces the
analytical trip points:

| Threshold | Min | Nom | Max | With 0.1 uA leakage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| UVLO rising | 3.925 | 3.987 | 4.069 | 3.863-4.131 |
| UVLO falling | 3.570 | 3.621 | 3.713 | 3.508-3.775 |
| OVLO rising | 5.648 | 5.738 | 5.858 | 5.589-5.917 |
| OVLO falling | 5.137 | 5.212 | 5.345 | 5.078-5.404 |

Nominal hysteresis: UVLO **0.365 V**, OVLO **0.526 V**. The OVLO min corner is **5.589 V**, only **0.089 V** above the 5.5 V USB maximum.

The state-machine sweep reproduces the nominal trip points: UVLO rise 3.9865 V, UVLO fall 3.6210 V, OVLO rise 5.7385 V, OVLO fall 5.2124 V. The 10 us sweep grid gives a sub-mV match to the divider arithmetic.

## 4. LED rail

Model: TPS2553-1 with R112 133 kohm (173.657/201.513/233.901 mA),
85 mohm typical Rds_on, 2 us current-limit response, 7.5 ms latch-off
deglitch, 11.7 uF effective output capacitance and a behavioral SK6805
constant-current load with a 3.0 V knee.

- Full white 1.14 A: the switch limits at **173.7 mA**, the rail collapses to **0.453 V** before latch (then 0 V), and the part latches off at **7.60 ms**. The electrical dissipation in U7 at that point is **0.616 W** (not a thermal result). Full white is not supported.
- 0.76 mA/ch = **173.28 mA** vs **173.657 mA** minimum ILIM: **0.377 mA (0.22%)** margin.
- With the LED load present at enable, the output-cap inrush pushes the rail into current limit; latched=True at 8.13 ms. With the load sequenced after the rail is up, latched=False and the steady rail is 3.9853 V (droop 0.0147 V).
- The 0.76 mA/ch cap is therefore an absolute edge, not a robust operating point. A **<=0.70 mA/ch** cap gives 14.06 mA margin, or the LED load must be sequenced after the rail is up.

## 5. Sequencing

| Rail | Time | Status |
|---|---:|---|
| VBUS | 0 ms | step |
| VBUS_FUSED | 0.00023 ms (RC) | simulated, 0.1 ohm source + C125 1 uF; 5 us simulation grid |
| VBUS_PROTECTED_RAW | 22.50 ms to 90% | simulated, eFuse 0.2 V/ms |
| +4V_REG | 32.11 ms to 90% | simulated, behavioral loop; monotonic=True |
| +3V3 | 32.53 ms (modeled) | **UNKNOWN LDO startup**; tau=0.5 ms assumption |
| +4V_LED | 51.1 ms (modeled) | firmware-controlled; modeled enable at 50 ms, not a board time |

The coupled eFuse+buck run gives 4 V 90% at **32.11 ms** and 99% at **33.42 ms** at 0.1 A, with largest drop from running max **1.90 mV** (monotonic=True). At 0.3 A the 90% time is **32.11 ms** and monotonic=True (largest drop from running max 1.90 mV).

The protected rail crosses the buck's 3.8 V VIN UVLO at **19.00 ms**; the buck soft-start then runs for 14.55 ms. The 4 V rail is monotonic in this model because the buck's soft-start reference is slower than the eFuse ramp and the loop tracks it. This is
conditional on the synthetic loop and the 29.7 uF effective output
capacitance; it is not a board measurement.

## What the simulation closes

- The eFuse dV/dt ramp time, capacitor-only inrush and stored energy under the stated cap/derating/ESR assumptions.
- The buck startup time, monotonicity and load-step direction/scale under the stated behavioral loop.
- The UVLO/OVLO trip points and hysteresis from the actual divider values.
- The LED rail current-limit/latch behavior and the 0.76 mA/ch margin.
- The rail order and the conditional 4 V monotonicity.

## What it cannot close

- **Stability**: the buck loop is a behavioral PI proxy, not the TPS62932 internal compensation. No phase-margin or loop-gain claim.
- **Thermal**: no junction-temperature model. The full-white U7 dissipation estimate is not a thermal result.
- **USB compliance**: no PD/BC1.2, cable, EMC or inrush compliance test.
- **Hardware qualification**: no bench measurement, no component tolerance qualification, no effective-capacitance measurement.
- **Actual LED V-I, LDO startup, firmware timing, eFuse thermal/current-limit behavior, buck PFM/DCM/foldback/hiccup.**

## Reproduction

```bash
.venv/bin/python pcb/tools/simulate_power.py
```

The script is deterministic, uses no network, and writes only under
`qa/power-sim/`. It sets `MPLCONFIGDIR` to `qa/power-sim/.mplconfig`.
