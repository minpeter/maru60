## Assumptions and frames

- Board Z: top **12.50 mm**, bottom **14.10 mm** (1.60 mm PCB; plate top 7.50 + 5.00 MX stack).
- Bottom-case cavity floor: **Z 18.20 mm** (`build_bottomcase_draft.FLOOR_ZP`).
- Full gasket travel: **0.80 mm** (the bottom-case build's stated travel).
- Board own frame -> plate frame: `x_plate = x + 0.4525`, `y_plate = 101.06775 - y` (`spec/boards.json`).
- Plate -> bottom-case world: `X_w = 142.95 - x_plate`, `Y_w = y_plate + 84.82` (`build_topcase_draft.pw`).
- The 3MF is in the bottom-case print frame. The print offset is recovered from the same build's world STL; the 3MF is then ray-cast directly. Frame error is 0.021 mm (mesh quantization).
- The battery-bay bottom-case 3MF is used. The relocated block is in front of the battery bay, so the no-battery-bay variant has the same cavity floor under it.

The 12.8 mm stock notch is **FAIL** for a 12.35 mm overmold: the stock notch is centred at plate X 19.35, the J1/plug at 18.45, so the plug's left edge is 0.67 mm outside the notch and would hit the case shell. The plug overmold height above the board is not in CAD, so the vertical plug fit cannot be concluded.

## What cannot be concluded from CAD

- Actual J3, U13, U11, U12 and 0603 body heights (the values used are datasheet/package nominals, not caliper readings).
- USB-C plug overmold height above the board and its actual width/corner radius (12.35 mm is the spec maximum).
- 7U stabilizer type (PCB-mount vs plate-mount) and housing dimensions; the plate 3MF is not available, only the DXF.
- Printed case floor Z and flatness (the CAD floor is 18.20; print tolerance and gasket compression are not modelled).
- Solder/paste thickness and component placement tolerance (the clearance numbers are nominal CAD, not worst-case assembly).
