## Assessment
The revised algorithm addresses the identified optimistic wrapper, third-party drill and implicit cross-layer/island shortcuts for the asserted actual board scope. Canonical UUID lookup precedes native layer/type/shape/cache handling. Each conductor's material is cut by every bounding-box-relevant actual through-hole; each zone is cut by the union of every such hole, regardless of an NPTH pad's copper layer membership. Compound primitives are simplified/unioned before island identity. Empty material creates no vertex, and separate object/layer/island vertices are not merged merely because their UUID is equal.

Plated barrel joins are separately guarded: true through span and four-layer membership, exactly one positive component per layer, native coordinate equality of global-hole material and own-hole annulus, and actual circular-via center/diameter or exact convex PTH hole enclosure. The coordinate comparator preserves winding and normalizes cyclic starts/outline order only; it no longer uses tiny Boolean difference-area zero as equality. ## Conservative contact criterion and remaining limits
Positive object/zone contact is accepted only if both material components, independently guarded inward2nm, still have positive native overlap. The original unguarded ledger and failed guard contacts remain explicit; failures are not joined or silently discarded from evidence. This is a conservative contact-confidence test, not a relaxation of plane/reference preservation or a transferable manufacturing tolerance. Positive fragments are retained even when they cannot prove an accepted edge.

Native connectivity remains candidate-pair discovery. The algorithm does not discover every possible geometric pair by exhaustive spatial search; that can omit a connection, rather than justify a false one. Native polygon/Boolean/island operations and the inward guard are not an exact rational proof of arbitrary continuous geometry. Source-specific resulting partitions, rejected/guard-failing contacts, tiny NPTH fragment participation and root witnesses still need examination in the fresh R2 board reports. No final no-impact or selected-board-validity conclusion is made before those reports finish.
